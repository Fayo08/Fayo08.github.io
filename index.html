Lagos Life: Besties

Lagos Life already has friends, chats, companies and rides across the city. I designed and built a feature that ties them together and gives players a reason to come back every day.

  • 🔥
    Chat streaks unlock a businessMessage a friend three days in a row and you can start a company together, owned by what each of you puts in.
  • 🚗
    Ride through Lagos togetherKeke, danfo, okada or a friend's car. A canvas-drawn drive with three camera views, a radio, and a go-slow on Third Mainland where you can buy gala from a hawker.
  • 🪩
    Land at real spotsi-Fitness, Quilox, Balogun Market and more, drawn as rooms where you and your friend stand together among other players.
  • 🤝
    Trust you earn, not buyChats, rides and activities together build trust, which unlocks perks like splitting the cab.
  • 🌗
    Day and nightThe whole world follows the in-game clock, and midnight starts a new day for streaks.
Under the hood
  • TypeScript and Vite, no framework. Game rules are pure functions, separate from the UI.
  • 36 tests on the rules: streaks, trust, fares, voting, ownership.
  • Exact money. Ownership is in basis points and profit splits use the largest-remainder method, so no naira is ever lost to rounding.
  • CI/CD with GitHub Actions: every push is tested, type-checked, built and deployed.
